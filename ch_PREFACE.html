Language is a doorway to a country’s people, its history and its heart. For many learners in India, the Sinhala language of Sri Lanka is a doorway that has remained closed, not because the language is impossible to learn, but because there are so few books with which to open it.
A new learner who looks for a Sinhala grammar book in an Indian bookshop, or searches for one on the internet, finds very little. There are a few phrase lists and some scattered notes, but hardly anything that explains Sinhala grammar (සිංහල ව්‍යාකරණ) step by step for a beginner who comes from an Indian-language background. This shortage makes the first steps unnecessarily difficult, and many learners lose heart before they have properly begun.
This book is my attempt to fill that gap. I have planned it as a guide for absolute beginners, moving slowly from the very basics towards advanced grammar. Every topic is explained in simple English and simple Sinhala, and Hindi meanings are given with the examples so that the learner can link the new language with one that is already familiar. Each lesson contains many examples, tables and diagrams, and ends with practice questions so that learners can check their own progress.
In preparing this book I have taken the help of an AI tool, together with the knowledge that I have acquired while studying Sinhala. An AI tool is only a helper; it is not the final authority on a language. There may therefore be some mistakes in spelling, in grammar rules or in the wording of examples. I request every reader, and especially teachers and native speakers of Sinhala, to double-check the contents carefully before relying on them.
Corrections and suggestions of any kind are most welcome, and they will be used to improve the book in later editions. I sincerely hope that this work will be a useful companion for new learners and will make the beautiful Sinhala language a little easier to approach.
Khem Chetry
