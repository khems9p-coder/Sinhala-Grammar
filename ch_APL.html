APPENDIX L
Appendix L: The National Anthem
ජාතික ගීය
राष्ट्रगान
The national anthem of Sri Lanka is ශ්‍රී ලංකා මාතා (Sri Lanka Matha, “Mother Sri Lanka”). It was written and composed by ආනන්ද සමරකෝන් (Ananda Samarakoon) and was officially adopted as the national anthem in 1951. It is sung in Sinhala, and a Tamil version is sung in Tamil-language schools and events.
REMEMBER — About the text
The words of the anthem are a modern copyrighted work, so this book does not print them. Please copy the official text from a government source, such as the Department of Government Information or your Sinhala teacher’s printed copy, and write it in the space below.

TIP — Etiquette
When the anthem is played, stand straight and quiet. It is sung at school assemblies, national days and official ceremonies. එය ගෞරවයෙන් ගායනා කරමු. – Let us sing it with respect.
