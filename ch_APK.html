REMEMBER — National animal?
Sri Lanka has no officially declared national animal. The elephant, the lion and the grizzled giant squirrel are often called national animals, but this is unofficial. Do not write this as a fact in an exam.
The emblem
The state emblem shows a lion holding a sword, inside a ring of lotus petals, standing on a grain-vase (full of rice) with the Dharma wheel and sun and moon beside it. The lion stands for the nation, the lotus for purity, rice for prosperity.

4. People and Religion

The largest groups are Sinhalese, Sri Lankan Tamils, Indian Tamils and Moors. The main religions are Buddhism, Hinduism, Islam and Christianity. Many festivals are national holidays for all.

Climate: tropical, warm all year; two monsoons bring rain. Vegetation: rain forest in the south-west, dry-zone forest in the north and east, cool montane forest in the hills, and mangroves on the coast. Work: farming (rice, tea, coconut, rubber, spices), garments, tourism, gem mining and fishing; tea and cinnamon are famous exports.

8. Sports, Art and Culture

Sports: cricket is the most loved game; volleyball is the national sport; elle, kabaddi and athletics are also popular. Dance: Kandyan (උඩරට), Low-country (පහතරට) and Sabaragamuwa dance. Festivals: Sinhala and Tamil New Year (අවුරුදු), Vesak, Poson, Esala Perahera, Deepavali, Eid and Christmas. Crafts: masks, batik, lace, brass and wood carving.
