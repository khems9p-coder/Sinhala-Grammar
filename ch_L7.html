Lesson 7: Singular and Plural
ඒකවචන සහ බහුවචන
एकवचन और बहुवचन
KEY IDEA — What you will learn in this lesson
1. Singular (ඒකවචන) and plural (බහුවචන) 2. Plurals of non-living nouns
3. Plurals of living nouns and the second plural form 4. Counting nouns: one book, two books

7.1 Singular and Plural

එක් අයෙකු හෝ එක් දෙයක් ඒකවචනයයි. එකකට වැඩි ගණන බහුවචනයයි.
ek ayeku ho ek deyak ekawachanayayi. ekakata waedi gana bahuwachanayayi.
One person or thing is singular. More than one is plural.
एक व्यक्ति या वस्तु एकवचन है। एक से अधिक बहुवचन है।
Sinhala makes plurals by changing the end of the noun. The way depends on whether the noun is non-living (Lesson 5) or living. Hindi works in a similar way (लड़का → लड़के).

7.2 Plurals of Non-living Nouns

RULE — Rule
Final -අ: add the sign ් (hal): පොත → පොත්.
Final -ය or -ව: drop it: මේසය → මේස, පුටුව → පුටු.

7.3 Plurals of Living Nouns (two forms)

TIP — Family words
Add -ලා to family words and names: අම්මා → අම්මලා (mothers), තාත්තා → තාත්තලා (fathers), අයියා → අයියලා, අක්කා → අක්කලා, මල්ලි → මල්ලිලා, යාළුවා → යාළුවලා.

After a number, the noun is plural and -ක් is added to the number for things: පොත් දෙකක්.
For people, the number takes -දෙනෙක්: දෙදෙනෙක්, තුන්දෙනෙක්.

7.7 Quick Revision (පුනරීක්ෂණය)

KEY IDEA — Remember these points
1. Non-living plural: final -අ takes ් (පොත්); final -ය / -ව drops (මේස, පුටු).
2. Living nouns have two plurals: subject form (ළමයෝ) and base form (ළමයින්).
3. Family words take -ලා: අම්මලා.
4. Numbers: පොත් දෙකක්, ළමයි දෙදෙනෙක්.

7.8 Practice Questions (අභ්‍යාස)

Part A: Non-living plurals
Q1. Make the plural: පොත, ගස, මේසය, පුටුව, ගෙදර.
Q2. Write the singular: මල්, ගම්, කාමර, ජනේල.
Part B: Living plurals
Q3. Write both plural forms: ළමයා, ගුරුවරයා, කුරුල්ලා.
Q4. Make the plural with -ලා: අම්මා, අක්කා, යාළුවා.
Q5. What is the plural of මිනිසා and ඇතා?
Part C: Counting and sentences
Q6. Write in Sinhala: two books, three flowers, five trees.
Q7. Write in Sinhala: one child, two children, three friends.
Q8. Translate into Sinhala: The children go to school.
Q9. Translate into English: මේසය උඩ පොත් දෙකක් තියෙනවා.
Q10. Write in Sinhala: four books, five children.
