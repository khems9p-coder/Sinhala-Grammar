Section A: Postpositions and particles (Lesson 23)
Q1. Give the Sinhala for: on, under, near, with, about, without.
Q2. Fill in: (a) බල්ලා ගස __ ඉන්නවා. (b) කෑමට __ අපි විවේක ගන්නවා.
Q3. Give the written partner: එක්ක, ගැන, වගේ.
Q4. Add a particle: (a) මම එනවා. (too) (b) අද එන්න. (itself)
Q5. Translate into Sinhala: This book was written by him.
Section B: Conjunctions, interjections, prefixes (Lesson 24)
Q6. Give the Sinhala for: and, or, but, therefore.
Q7. Write the “if” form of: ආවා, කළා.
Q8. Translate into Sinhala: Although it rained, we went.
Q9. Which interjection shows (a) pain (b) admiration?
Q10. Give the meaning of the prefixes සු-, දුර්-, නිර්- with one word each.
Section C: Sentence patterns (Lesson 25)
Q11. Translate into Sinhala: (a) I have a book. (b) I am thirsty. (c) I want to go.
Q12. Translate into Sinhala: the child who reads the book.
Q13. Correct (written): (a) මම පොතක් කියවයි. (b) ඔවුහු ගමට යමු.
Q14. Write in written Sinhala: He is my friend.
Q15. Name the three kinds of sentences.
Section D: Transformations (Lesson 26)
Q16. Make negative: (a) ඔහු ආවා. (b) ඇය ගුරුවරියක්.
Q17. Make a question with a question word: ඔහු කොළඹ යනවා.
Q18. Change to plural (written): සොල්දාදුවා රට රකියි.
Q19. Change into the passive: රජය පාලමක් ඉදි කළේය.
Q20. Change into written Sinhala: එයාලා වැඩ කරලා තියෙනවා.
Q21. Write the causative of: කරනවා, හදනවා.
Section E: Reported speech (Lesson 27)
Q22. Translate into Sinhala (spoken): He said, “I am coming.”
Q23. Change into indirect speech: ඇය “මම ගියා” කියලා කිව්වා.
Q24. Report: ගුරුතුමා: “ඔබේ නම මොකක්ද?”
Q25. Report (written): නිලධාරියා: “ඉදිරියට යන්න.”
