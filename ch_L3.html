3.1 What Is a Vowel Sign? (පිළි)

ව්‍යඤ්ජනයකට ස්වරයක් එක් කරන්නේ පිල්ලකිනි.
wyanjanayakata swarayak ek karanne pillakini.
A vowel is added to a consonant by a vowel sign.
व्यंजन में स्वर मात्रा से जुड़ता है।
You know that ක carries the vowel “a”. To say kaa, ki or ku we do not write a new letter. We keep the consonant and add a small mark called a පිල්ල (pilla, plural පිළි, pili). Hindi calls it मात्रा. The idea is the same, and many shapes are different.
The vowel signs are written before, after, above, below or on both sides of the consonant:

Hindi also has a sign that goes in front (ि in कि) and signs above and below (े, ु). Sinhala follows the same idea, but the signs look different.

REMEMBER — Two shapes, one sound
ො and ෝ look like ෙ + ා. They are written as one sign around the consonant. Never write ෙ and ා as two separate things.

3.7 Common Spelling Mistakes

#
Mistake
Advice
1
Confusing ඉ / ඊ and උ / ඌ
Short sound = no extra length; long sound = held. Check each word in a dictionary at first.
2
Confusing ණ / න and ල / ළ
They sound alike. Learn the spelling with the word, e.g. කළු (black), නිවස (house).
3
Confusing ශ / ෂ / ස
Learn the spelling with the word, e.g. ශිෂ්‍යයා, සිංහල, ගුරුවරයා.
4
Writing ෙ after the letter
ෙ ේ ෛ always come before the letter.
5
Forgetting ් (hal)
A final consonant needs ් (hal): බත් (rice), ගස් (trees). Always check the end of the word.
6
Splitting ක්‍ර, ව්‍ය, ශ්‍ර
These are joined forms. Type or write them as one unit.

3.9 Quick Revision (පුනරීක්ෂණය)

KEY IDEA — Remember these points
1. A vowel sign (පිල්ල) changes the vowel of a consonant: ක + ා = කා.
2. ෙ ේ ෛ go before; ා ැ ෑ after; ි ී above; ු ූ ෘ below; ො ෝ ෞ on both sides.
3. ්‍ර (රකාරාංශය) and ්‍ය (යංශය) are short forms of ර and ය joined below a letter; ර් (රේඵය) goes above.
4. Double consonants such as ක්ක, ම්ම, ත්ත are very common: අක්කා, අම්මා, තාත්තා.
5. Many spellings are the same as in Hindi: ක්‍රියා, ප්‍රශ්න, ව්‍යාකරණ.
