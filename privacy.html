Privacy Policy

Sinhala Grammar for Beginners · Developer: Khem Chetry · Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: the app collects no personal information, needs no account, shows no advertisements and sends nothing about you to anyone.

1. What this app is

Sinhala Grammar for Beginners is an offline reading app for learning Sinhala grammar. All lessons, exercises and appendices are stored inside the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any other permission.

3. Information stored on your device

To make reading comfortable, the app remembers a few things only on your own phone: the last chapter you opened and where you stopped, which chapters you have finished, the places you saved with the star, and your text-size and theme choices. This information never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time with About → Reset progress, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no tracking and no third-party software that collects data.

5. Internet use

The app works completely offline. Links that you choose to tap (for example an e-mail address or a website) open in another app on your phone, which has its own privacy policy.

6. Children

The app is an educational reference for learners of all ages. Because it collects no information at all, it does not collect information from children.

7. Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data on our side to protect. The few settings stored on your phone are protected by your phone's own security.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer, Khem Chetry.
