Thirty-nine phrases to use during a visit. The line under each Sinhala phrase shows how to read it in Roman letters. Practise aloud and smile; Sri Lankans are delighted when visitors try their language.

KEY IDEA — Remember
Use ඔව් (yes) and නැහැ (no). Politeness: add කරුණාකර (please) at the start and ස්තූතියි at the end. To get attention, say සමාවෙන්න.
