4.1 Syllables (අක්ෂර)

වචනයක් කොටස් වලට බෙදා කියන්නට පුළුවන්.
wachanayak kotas walata bedaa kiyannata puluwan.
A word can be divided into parts when we speak it.
किसी शब्द को बोलते समय भागों में बाँटा जा सकता है।
A word is made of one or more syllables, the beats you hear when you say it slowly. In Sinhala writing, one letter with its vowel sign is usually one syllable. To count syllables, clap once for each beat.

TIP — Why syllables matter
In Lesson 5 onwards you will add endings to words (ළමයා + ට = ළමයාට). Knowing where one syllable ends and the next begins helps you add the ending without spelling mistakes.

KEY IDEA — A learner’s trick
When you meet a new Sinhala word, ask: “Is there a Hindi word that sounds a little like this?” If the answer is yes, the word is probably easy to remember. If no, learn it as a new word.

4.8 Quick Revision (පුනරීක්ෂණය)

KEY IDEA — Remember these points
1. A syllable is one beat of a word; ළමයා has three (ළ – ම – යා).
2. Words are of four kinds by origin: තත්සම, තද්භව, දේශීය, විදේශීය (Hindi: तत्सम, तद्भव, देशज, विदेशी).
3. Many Sinhala and Hindi words are cousins from the same Sanskrit word: ගම / गाँव, අත / हाथ.
4. A word has a root and an ending: ගම + ට = ගමට.
5. Two words can join as a compound (සමාස): රජ + මාළිගාව = රජමාළිගාව.

4.9 Practice Questions (අභ්‍යාස)

Part A: Syllables
Q1. Count the syllables: (a) ගම (b) පාසල (c) ගුරුවරයා (d) ශිෂ්‍යයා.
Q2. Divide into syllables: ළමයා, ක්‍රීඩාව.
Part B: Origin of words
Q3. Name the four kinds of words by origin, in English and Hindi.
Q4. Which kind is each? (a) ජලය (b) අත (c) බස් (d) අම්මා.
Q5. Give the Hindi word that is related to: ගම, අත, ඇස, දත.
Part C: Building words
Q6. Add the ending: (a) ගම + ට (b) ළමයා + ගේ (c) පොත + ක්.
Q7. Break each word into root and ending: ගමෙන්, පොතක්, යනවා.
Q8. Make a compound word from: (a) පොත් + හල (b) මහ + පාර. Give the English meaning.
Q9. Write four words from the “write” family, with their English meanings.
Q10. Translate into Sinhala: (a) The child goes to the village. (b) The teacher reads a book.
