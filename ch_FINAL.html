Instructions
Time: 2 hours. Total: 100 marks. Questions 1–40 carry 2 marks each (80 marks). Question 41 (essay) carries 12 marks and Question 42 (letter) carries 8 marks.
Grades: 85 and above = Excellent (විශිෂ්ට); 70–84 = Very good (ඉතා හොඳ); 50–69 = Pass (සමත්); below 50 = revise and try again.
Section A: Foundations (Lessons 1–4)
Q1. What is the usual word order of a Sinhala sentence?
Q2. How many vowels and consonants has the traditional Sinhala alphabet?
Q3. Write: ක + ා, ම + ි, ත + ු, ප + ො.
Q4. Name the four kinds of words by origin.
Section B: Nouns, pronouns, adjectives (Lessons 5–9)
Q5. Write the indefinite form (“a …”): පොත, ළමයා, ගුරුවරයා.
Q6. Write the plural: පොත, ගස, ළමයා, බල්ලා.
Q7. Write the feminine: ගුරුවරයා, ශිෂ්‍යයා, පුතා.
Q8. Write the plural pronouns of: මම, ඔහු, එය.
Q9. Translate into Sinhala: a big house; my book.
Section C: The cases (Lessons 10–15)
Q10. Name the eight cases in Sinhala or English.
Q11. Add the ending: (a) ළමයා + “to” (b) ගම + “from” (c) ගුරුතුමා + “of” (d) පාසල + “in”.
Q12. Which case is the underlined word in? මම පෑනෙන් ලියනවා.
Q13. Translate into Sinhala: I gave a book to the teacher.
Q14. Translate into Sinhala: He came from the village.
Q15. Write the vocative (calling form): පුතා, අම්මා, මිත්‍රවරු.
Section D: Verbs and tenses (Lessons 16–22)
Q16. Write the spoken past: යනවා, එනවා, කරනවා, කනවා, දෙනවා.
Q17. Conjugate යනවා in the written present with මම, අපි, ඔහු, ඔවුහු.
Q18. Write the written past of කළා with මම, ඔහු, ඇය.
Q19. Translate into Sinhala (spoken): I will come tomorrow. (a promise)
Q20. Translate into Sinhala (spoken): I am reading; I have read.
Q21. Make negative: (a) මම යනවා. (b) ඔහු ගියා.
Q22. Make a question: ඔබ සිංහල දන්නවා.
Q23. Write in Sinhala: Please sit down. Do not talk.
Q24. Make adverbs from: ලස්සන, සතුට.
Section E: Linking words and sentences (Lessons 23–27)
Q25. Give the Sinhala for: on, under, with, about, because of.
Q26. Add the particle: (a) මම එනවා. (too) (b) අද එන්න. (itself)
Q27. Translate into Sinhala: If you come, I shall be happy.
Q28. Translate into Sinhala: (a) I am hungry. (b) I have a book.
Q29. Translate into Sinhala: the letter that I wrote.
Q30. Change into the passive: ඔහු ලිපියක් ලිව්වේය.
Q31. Change into indirect speech: ඔහු “මම එනවා” කියලා කිව්වා.
Q32. Correct (written): (a) මම පොතක් කියවයි. (b) ළමයි පාසලට යයි.
Section F: Styles, translation and writing (Lessons 28–32)
Q33. Give the written word for: ගෙදර, වතුර, සල්ලි, එක්ක, ගැන.
Q34. Change into written Sinhala: (a) එයා ඊයේ ආවා. (b) මට සල්ලි නැහැ.
Q35. Translate into English: අයදුම්පත් කැඳවනු ලැබේ.
Q36. Translate into English: රැස්වීම ලබන සතියේ පැවැත්වෙනු ඇත.
Q37. Translate into Sinhala: Answer all the questions.
Q38. Translate into written Sinhala: Sri Lanka is a beautiful island.
Q39. How do you greet and end a formal letter to a male officer?
Q40. Name the three parts of an essay in Sinhala.
Section G: Writing
Q41. Write an essay of about 100 words on one of these: මගේ රට / මගේ පවුල / සොල්දාදුවෙකුගේ ජීවිතය. [12 marks]
Q42. Write an application to the head of your institution asking for five days’ leave. [8 marks]
