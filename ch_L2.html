2.1 Sounds and Letters (ශබ්ද සහ අක්ෂර)

අපි කථා කරන්නේ ශබ්ද වලිනි. අපි ලියන්නේ අක්ෂර වලිනි.
api kathaa karanne shabda walini. api liyanne akshara walini.
We speak with sounds. We write with letters.
हम ध्वनियों से बोलते हैं। हम अक्षरों से लिखते हैं।
The full set of letters of a language is its alphabet. In Sinhala it is called අක්ෂරමාලාව (aksharamaalaawa, “the garland of letters”) or හෝඩිය (hoodiya). Hindi calls it वर्णमाला. Five facts about the Sinhala script will save you much trouble:
Left to right. Sinhala is written from left to right, like English and Hindi.
No capital letters. There is no difference between “big” and “small” letters. Names such as කොළඹ (Colombo) are written like any other word.
No headline. Hindi letters hang from a line (शिरोरेखा); Sinhala letters do not. They are round and written freely.
An abugida. A consonant letter already carries the vowel “a”: ක is ka, not just k. To change the vowel we add a sign (Lesson 3).
One letter, one sound. Sinhala spelling is regular: each letter has one sound, so once you know the alphabet you can read any word.

2.2 The Vowels (ස්වර)

A vowel (ස්වරය, Hindi: स्वर) can be spoken by itself. The traditional alphabet has 18 vowels. They come in short and long pairs. A short vowel is called හ්‍රස්ව (hrasva, short) and a long vowel දීර්ඝ (diirgha, long). Hindi uses the same two words: ह्रस्व and दीर्घ.

RULE — How to remember the vowels
• Ten everyday vowels: අ ආ ඇ ඈ ඉ ඊ උ ඌ එ ඒ ඔ ඕ (twelve, counting the pairs) – learn these first.
• Two diphthongs (two vowel sounds joined): ඓ = a + i, ඖ = a + u.
• Four Sanskrit vowels: ඍ ඎ ඏ ඐ. Only ඍ is seen, in a few Sanskrit words such as ඍෂි. Hindi-speakers know ऋ already.
• Sinhala has the two vowels ඇ and ඈ, which Hindi has no letter for. Say them like the “a” in the English word cat.
TIP — Short “e” and “o”
Hindi has only one ए and one ओ. Sinhala has two of each: short එ, ඔ and long ඒ, ඕ. Listen: එක (eka, one) is quick; ඒක (eeka, that) is held longer.

Look at the third sign. ක is ka. When we put ් on it, ක් is only k. Words may end in such a letter: බත් (bath, rice), ගස් (gas, trees), පොත් (poth, books). This sign also helps us make plurals (Lesson 7).
REMEMBER — ං or ඞ?
The sign ං is a “dot” (බිංදුව) added to a vowel or consonant. The letter ඞ is a full consonant of the ක group. Do not mix them up.

KEY IDEA — Count the letters
25 (five groups) + 4 (ය ර ල ව) + 4 (ශ ෂ ස හ) + ළ + ෆ + ඥ + 5 prenasalised = 41 consonants.
18 vowels + 41 consonants = 59 letters. Everyday modern Sinhala uses fewer of them; the rest appear mostly in words from Sanskrit, Pali and foreign languages.

TIP — Study tip for Hindi speakers
Learn Sinhala letters in the same order as your Hindi varnamala: ක ඛ ග ඝ ඞ — क ख ग घ ङ. Say both aloud, one pair at a time. Within a week you will know the 25 letters of the five groups.

2.8 Quick Revision (පුනරීක්ෂණය)

KEY IDEA — Remember these points
1. Sinhala is written left to right, has no capital letters, and each consonant carries the vowel “a”.
2. There are 18 vowels in the traditional alphabet: short, long, and two diphthongs (ඓ, ඖ).
3. There are 41 consonants; the first 25 are in five groups, just as in Hindi.
4. ං adds a nasal sound; ඃ adds a breath; ් removes the vowel.
5. Aspirated letters (ඛ, ඝ, ඵ …) are written but not breathed out in modern speech.
6. ඇ and ඈ have no Hindi letters; say them like the “a” in cat.

2.9 Practice Questions (අභ්‍යාස)

Part A: The vowels
Q1. How many vowels and how many consonants are there in the traditional Sinhala alphabet?

Part B: The consonants
Q4. Write the Hindi letter for each: ක, ග, ත, ප, ම, ස.
Q5. Give the name of the group (in English) for each: ච, ට, ත, ප, ක.
Q6. Write the five prenasalised consonants.
Q7. Which of these are aspirated letters? ක, ඛ, ප, ඵ, ග, ඝ.
