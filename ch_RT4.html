Section A: Verbs (Lesson 16)
Q1. Write the stem of: බලනවා, කරනවා, ලියනවා.
Q2. Transitive or intransitive? කියවනවා, නිදාගන්නවා, දෙනවා, දුවනවා.
Q3. Write the “happening” partner: කඩනවා, අරිනවා.
Q4. Fill in ඉන්නවා or තියෙනවා: (a) සොල්දාදුවා කඳවුරේ __. (b) පොත බෑගයේ __.
Section B: Present tense (Lesson 17)
Q5. Write the six written present endings.
Q6. Conjugate බලනවා in the written present with මම, අපි, ඔහු, ඔවුහු.
Q7. Change into written Sinhala: (a) මම බත් කනවා. (b) ළමයි කිරි බොනවා.
Q8. Correct: (a) අපි යයි. (b) ඔහු කරමි.
Section C: Past tense (Lesson 18)
Q9. Write the spoken past: යනවා, එනවා, කරනවා, බලනවා, දෙනවා, ගන්නවා.
Q10. Write the written past of ගියා with: මම, ඔහු, ඇය, ඔවුහු.
Q11. Change into written Sinhala: (a) ඔහු පොතක් කියෙව්වා. (b) ඇය ගෙදර ආවා.
Q12. Translate into Sinhala (spoken): I drank tea yesterday.
Section D: Future (Lesson 19)
Q13. Write the -න්නම් form: එනවා, කරනවා, කියනවා.
Q14. Change into the -නු ඇත form: ඇමතිතුමා හෙට පැමිණෙනවා.
Q15. Translate into Sinhala: (a) I will come tomorrow. (b) He will probably come next week.
Section E: Continuous and perfect (Lesson 20)
Q16. Write the spoken “having done” form: කනවා, යනවා, එනවා, ලියනවා.
Q17. Translate into Sinhala (spoken): (a) I am reading. (b) We have finished the work.
Q18. Join into one sentence: ඔහු කඩේට ගියා. ඔහු පාන් ගත්තා.
Q19. Give the written Sinhala for: (a) has written (b) was writing.
Section F: Negatives, questions, commands (Lesson 21)
Q20. Make negative: (a) මම දන්නවා. (b) ඔහු ගියා. (c) මම ගුරුවරයෙක්.
Q21. Turn into a question: ඔබ හෙට එනවා.
Q22. Write the Sinhala for: who, where, when, why, how much.
Q23. Write in Sinhala: (a) Please sit down. (b) Do not go there. (c) Let us eat.
Section G: Adverbs (Lesson 22)
Q24. Make adverbs from: ලස්සන, සතුට, පැහැදිලි.
Q25. Translate into Sinhala: She speaks Sinhala well.
