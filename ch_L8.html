8.1 What Is a Pronoun?

නාමයක් වෙනුවට යෙදෙන පදය සර්වනාමයයි.
naamayak wenuwata yedena padaya sarwanaamayayi.
A word used in place of a noun is a pronoun.
संज्ञा के स्थान पर प्रयुक्त शब्द सर्वनाम कहलाता है।
Instead of repeating “Nimal”, we say “he”. In Sinhala, pronouns are divided into three persons: the speaker (1st), the person spoken to (2nd), and the person spoken about (3rd). Pronouns are the first words every learner must memorise.

KEY IDEA — Remember
ඔහු is used for males, ඇය for females and එය for things. ඔවුන් (they) is used for people of both sexes.

8.8 Quick Revision (පුනරීක්ෂණය)

KEY IDEA — Remember these points
1. Persons: I / we (1st), you (2nd), he / she / it / they (3rd).
2. ඔහු = he, ඇය = she, එය = it, ඔවුන් = they.
3. Use ඔබ to strangers; ඔයා only to friends; avoid තෝ.
4. මේ / ඒ / අර = this / that / that over there.
5. Forms: මම – මාව – මට – මගේ.

8.9 Practice Questions (අභ්‍යාස)

Part A: Personal pronouns
Q1. Write the Sinhala pronouns for: I, we, he, she, they.
Q2. Which pronoun is used for things?
Q3. Which word for “you” is safest for a learner talking to a stranger?
Part B: Forms and question words
Q4. Write the forms: (a) my (b) to him (c) her (d) to us.
Q5. Write in Sinhala: who? what? where? why?
Q6. Give the written form of: මේක, ඒක, මොකක්ද.
Part C: Sentences
Q7. Translate into Sinhala: (a) We go to school. (b) She reads a book.
Q8. Translate into Sinhala: What is your name? My name is Nimal.
Q9. Translate into English: මට පොතක් ඕනෑ.
Q10. Translate into English: ඔවුන් ගමට යනවා.
