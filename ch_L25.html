25.1 The Parts of a Sentence

සම්පූර්ණ අදහසක් ප්‍රකාශ කරන පද සමූහය වාක්‍යය නම් වේ.
sampuurna adahasak prakaasha karana pada samuuhaya waakyaya nam we.
A group of words that expresses a complete idea is a sentence.
पूरा विचार प्रकट करने वाला शब्द-समूह वाक्य कहलाता है।

Everything else is added around this frame: describing words stand before the word they describe, and the verb stays last. A longer sentence usually runs: Subject – time – place – receiver – object – manner – verb.
One long sentence
ගුරුතුමා ඊයේ පන්තියේදී ළමයාට පොතක් සතුටින් දුන්නා.
guruthumaa iiye panthiyeedii lamayaata pothak sathutin dunnaa.
The teacher happily gave a book to the child in the class yesterday.
गुरुजी ने कल कक्षा में बच्चे को ख़ुशी से एक किताब दी।

25.2 Ten Basic Sentence Patterns

#
Pattern
Sinhala
English · Hindi
1
Subject + verb
ළමයා නිදාගන්නවා.
lamayaa nidaagannawaa.
The child sleeps. · बच्चा सोता है।
2
Subject + object + verb
මම පොතක් කියවනවා.
mama pothak kiyawanawaa.
I read a book. · मैं किताब पढ़ता हूँ।
3
Subject + place + verb
අපි ගමට යනවා.
api gamata yanawaa.
We go to the village. · हम गाँव जाते हैं।
4
Subject + receiver + object + verb
අම්මා මට තේ දුන්නා.
ammaa mata the dunnaa.
Mother gave me tea. · माँ ने मुझे चाय दी।
5
A is B (noun)
මම සොල්දාදුවෙක්.
mama soldaaduwek.
I am a soldier. · मैं सैनिक हूँ।
6
A is B (adjective + යි)
ගෙදර ලොකුයි.
gedara lokuyi.
The house is big. · घर बड़ा है।
7
There is …
මේසය උඩ පොතක් තියෙනවා.
meesaya uda pothak thiyenawaa.
There is a book on the table. · मेज़ पर एक किताब है।
8
To me there is … (= I have)
මට පොතක් තියෙනවා.
mata pothak thiyenawaa.
I have a book. · मेरे पास एक किताब है।
9
To me … (feeling, ability)
මට බඩගිනියි.
mata badaginiyi.
I am hungry. · मुझे भूख लगी है।
10
Stress pattern (-න්නේ)
මම යන්නේ ගෙදර.
mama yanne gedara.
It is home that I am going to. · मैं घर ही जा रहा हूँ।
RULE — “A is B” in written Sinhala
Written Sinhala adds an ending to the last word: ඔහු ගුරුවරයෙකි. (He is a teacher.) මෙය පොතකි. (This is a book.) ඔහු මගේ මිතුරාය. (He is my friend.) ගෙදර විශාලය. (The house is large.) With මම the ending is -මි: මම සොල්දාදුවෙක්මි. (I am a soldier.)

25.3 Sentences with මට (“to me”)

Hindi says मुझे भूख लगी है, मुझे हिंदी आती है, मेरे पास किताब है. Sinhala uses exactly the same idea: the person stands in the dative (-ට). A Hindi-speaker finds this natural; an English-speaker must learn it.

25.4 Describing a Noun with a Verb

English says “the child who reads the book”. Sinhala has no word for “who” or “which” here. It puts the verb, in its describing form, before the noun, like an adjective. Hindi can do the same: किताब पढ़ने वाला बच्चा.

25.8 Quick Revision (පුනරීක්ෂණය)

KEY IDEA — Remember these points
1. Frame: subject – object – verb; describing words come before; the verb is last.
2. “A is B” needs no verb in speech: මම සොල්දාදුවෙක්. Written: ඔහු ගුරුවරයෙකි.
3. “I have / want / can / feel” use මට: මට පොතක් තියෙනවා, මට ඕනෑ, මට පුළුවන්.
4. No “who / which”: the describing verb comes before the noun: පොත කියවන ළමයා.
5. Written verbs agree: මම යමි, අපි යමු, ඔහු යයි, ඔවුහු යති.
6. Three kinds: සරල, සංයුක්ත, මිශ්‍ර.
