APPENDIX M
Appendix M: Amazing Facts about Sri Lanka
ශ්‍රී ලංකාව ගැන පුදුමාකාර කරුණු
श्रीलंका के रोचक तथ्य
Here are twenty facts to surprise your friends. Each can be a topic for a short talk in Sinhala.
#
Fact
Sinhala keyword
1
The Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi at Anuradhapura grew from a cutting brought in the 3rd century BCE; it is among the oldest historically recorded planted trees.
ජය ශ්‍රී මහා බෝධිය
jaya shrii mahaa boodhiya
2
Sirimavo Bandaranaike became the world’s first woman Prime Minister in 1960.
ප්‍රථම කාන්තා අගමැති
prathama kaanthaa agamaethi
3
The word “serendipity” comes from Serendib, an old name of the island.
සෙරන්ඩිබ්
serandib
4
Ancient kings built huge irrigation tanks; the Parakrama Samudra is a famous one.
වැව්
waew
5
Sigiriya, a rock fortress of the 5th century, rises about 200 m.
සීගිරිය
siigiriya
6
The Lotus Tower in Colombo is among the tallest towers in South Asia.
නෙළුම් කුළුණ
nelum kuluna
7
Blue whales can be seen from the south coast, near Mirissa.
නිල් තල්මසා
nil thalmasaa
8
Adam’s Bridge, a chain of shoals, nearly joins Sri Lanka to India.
ආදම්ගේ පාලම
aadamge paalama
9
Mahinda and Sanghamitta from India brought Buddhism to the island.
මහින්ද හා සඟමිත්තා
mahinda haa sangamiththaa
10
Tea was first grown on a large scale in the 1860s; “Ceylon tea” is world famous.
ලංකා තේ
lankaa the
11
Sri Lanka is the main source of true cinnamon.
කුරුඳු
kurundu
12
Yala National Park has one of the densest leopard populations known.
දිවියා
diwiyaa
13
Sinhala letters are round because they were once written on palm leaves with a pointed stylus.
පුස්කොළ පොත්
puskola poth
14
Sri Pada (Adam’s Peak) is a pilgrimage place for several faiths.
ශ්‍රී පාදය
shrii paadaya
15
Cars keep to the left side of the road.
වමට
wamata
16
The Esala Perahera of Kandy features decorated elephants and dancers.
ඇසල පෙරහැර
aesala perahaera
17
Pinnawala has an elephant orphanage.
පින්නවල
pinnawala
18
Sri Lanka won the Cricket World Cup in 1996.
ක්‍රිකට්
krikat
19
The island has a high literacy rate, above 90%.
සාක්ෂරතාව
saaksharathaawa
20
Sri Lanka has eight UNESCO World Heritage Sites.
ලෝක උරුමය
looka urumaya
TIP — Talk in Sinhala
Choose one fact and say it aloud: මේක ශ්‍රී ලංකාව ගැන පුදුම කරුණක්. – This is an amazing fact about Sri Lanka.
