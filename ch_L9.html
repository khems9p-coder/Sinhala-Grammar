9.1 What Is an Adjective?

නාමයක ගුණය හෝ ස්වභාවය දක්වන පදය විශේෂණයයි.
naamayaka gunaya ho swabhaawaya dakwana padaya wisheshanayayi.
A word that tells the quality of a noun is an adjective.
संज्ञा की विशेषता बताने वाला शब्द विशेषण कहलाता है।
KEY IDEA — Two good rules
1. The adjective comes before the noun: ලස්සන මල (beautiful flower), as in Hindi: सुंदर फूल.
2. The adjective never changes for gender or number. Hindi: अच्छा लड़का, अच्छी लड़की. Sinhala: හොඳ ළමයා, හොඳ ළමිය, හොඳ ළමයි.

9.4 Adjective in the Predicate: the Ending -යි

When the adjective comes after the noun and says “is …”, we add -යි to it (spoken):
#
Sinhala
English
Hindi
1
මේ මල ලස්සනයි.
mee mala lassanayi
This flower is beautiful.
यह फूल सुंदर है।
2
ගස උසයි.
gasa usayi
The tree is tall.
पेड़ ऊँचा है।
3
ගෙදර ලොකුයි.
gedara lokuyi
The house is big.
घर बड़ा है।
4
වතුර සීතලයි.
watura siithalayi
The water is cold.
पानी ठंडा है।
5
පොත අළුත්.
potha alut
The book is new.
किताब नई है।
TIP — Written style
Written Sinhala usually adds -ය or -යි: මෙම මල ලස්සනය / ලස්සනයි.

9.5 Comparing: -er and -est

Degree
Rule
Example
Comparative (more)
“than” + ට + වඩා
ඔහු ඇයට වඩා උසයි.
ohu aeyata wadaa usayi – He is taller than her.

ඉතාම or සියල්ලටම වඩා

9.7 Quick Revision (පුනරීක්ෂණය)

KEY IDEA — Remember these points
1. The adjective comes before the noun.
2. It does not change for gender or number.
3. After the noun, add -යි: මල ලස්සනයි.
4. Compare with ට … වඩා; superlative with ඉතාම.

9.8 Practice Questions (අභ්‍යාස)

Part A: Adjectives
Q1. Write the opposite: ලොකු, උස, හොඳ, අළුත්, සීතල.
Q2. Write in Sinhala: red, blue, green, white, black.
Q3. Where does an adjective stand, before or after the noun? Give one example.
Part B: Using adjectives
Q4. Translate into Sinhala: (a) a beautiful flower (b) a big house (c) three new books.
Q5. Make a sentence with -යි: (tree, tall).
Q6. Does the adjective change for gender? Compare Hindi अच්छा / अच්छी.
Part C: Comparing
Q7. Translate into Sinhala: He is taller than her.
Q8. Translate into Sinhala: This tree is the tallest.
Q9. Translate into English: මේ පොත ඒකට වඩා ලොකුයි.
Q10. Translate into English: ගෙදර ලොකුයි.
