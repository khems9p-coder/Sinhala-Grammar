21.1 Saying “Not” with Verbs

Change the verb to its -න්නේ form (present) or -ේ form (past) and add නැහැ (often said නෑ). The form is the same for every person.
#
Positive
Negative
English · Hindi
1
මම යනවා.
mama yanawaa.
මම යන්නේ නැහැ.
mama yanne naehae.
I do not go. · मैं नहीं जाता।
2
ඔහු කනවා.

ඔහු කන්නේ නැහැ.
ohu kanne naehae.
He does not eat. · वह नहीं खाता।
3
අපි දන්නවා.

21.2 “Not” with Nouns and Adjectives

To say that something is not a thing or a quality, put නෙවෙයි (written නොවේ) after the word.
#
Sinhala
English
Hindi
1
මම ගුරුවරයෙක් නෙවෙයි.
mama guruwarayek neweyi.
I am not a teacher.
मैं शिक्षक नहीं हूँ।
2
මේක මගේ පොත නෙවෙයි.
meeka mage potha neweyi.
This is not my book.
यह मेरी किताब नहीं है।
3
ඒ ගෙදර ලොකු නැහැ.
ee gedara loku naehae.
That house is not big.
वह घर बड़ा नहीं है।
4
මෙය පහසු කාර්‍යයක් නොවේ.
meya pahasu kaaryayak nowe.
This is not an easy task. (written)
यह आसान काम नहीं है।

21.4 Yes–No Questions with ද

Add ද to the end of the sentence. The word order does not change. This is like the Hindi क्या, but it comes at the end.
#
Sinhala
English
Hindi
1
ඔබ සිංහල දන්නවාද?
oba sinhala dannawaada?
Do you know Sinhala?
क्या आप सिंहली जानते हैं?
2
ඔහු හෙට එනවාද?
ohu heta enawaada?
Is he coming tomorrow?
क्या वह कल आएगा?
3
ඔයා බත් කෑවාද?
oyaa bath kaaewaada?
Did you eat rice?
क्या तुमने चावल खाया?
4
මේ ඔබේ පොතද?
me obe pothada?
Is this your book?
क्या यह आपकी किताब है?
5
ඔබ ගුරුවරයෙක්ද?
oba guruwarayekda?
Are you a teacher?
क्या आप शिक्षक हैं?
6
තේ තියෙනවාද?
the thiyenawaada?
Is there tea?
क्या चाय है?
TIP — Short answers
Yes = ඔව්. No = නැහැ. Often the verb itself is the answer: “එනවාද?” — “එනවා.” (“Coming?” — “Coming.”)

21.9 Quick Revision (පුනරීක්ෂණය)

KEY IDEA — Remember these points
1. Verb negative: -න්නේ නැහැ (present), -ේ නැහැ (past): යන්නේ නැහැ, ගියේ නැහැ.
2. Noun / adjective negative: නෙවෙයි (written නොවේ).
3. Written negative: නො- before the verb: නොයමි, නොදනිති.
4. Yes–no question: add ද at the end: එනවාද?
5. Question words: කවුද, මොකක්ද, කොහේද, කවදාද, ඇයි, කොහොමද, කීයද.
6. Request: -න්න. Let us: -මු. Don’t: -න්න එපා.

21.10 Practice Questions (අභ්‍යාස)

Part A: Negatives
Q1. Make negative (spoken): (a) මම යනවා. (b) ඔහු ආවා. (c) අපි දන්නවා.
Q2. Make negative: (a) මම සොල්දාදුවෙක්. (b) මේක මගේ පෑන.
Q3. Make negative (written): (a) මම යමි. (b) ඔවුහු දනිති.
Q4. Give the opposite: පුළුවන්, ඕනෑ, ඔව්.
Part B: Questions
Q5. Turn into yes–no questions: (a) ඔබ සිංහල දන්නවා. (b) ඔහු ගියා.
Q6. Write the Sinhala question words for: who, what, where, when, why, how.
Q7. Ask a question for the underlined part: ඔහු කොළඹට යනවා.
Part C: Commands
Q8. Write polite requests: come, sit down, look, write.
Q9. Write in Sinhala: (a) Let us go. (b) Do not talk. (c) Please sit down.
Q10. Translate into English: සියලු ප්‍රශ්නවලට පිළිතුරු ලියන්න.
